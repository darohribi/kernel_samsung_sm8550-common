# =========================================================================
# 9. FIRMWARE MODIFICATIONS (if accessible)
# =========================================================================

## a) RPMh (Resource Power Manager, hardware)
## These are typically in vendor firmware; document for reference:
## - Raise PMIC voltage ceiling for higher CPU/GPU clocks
## - Increase sustained power budget (from 8W to 12W+)
## - Disable thermal emergency shutdown at 100°C (move to 105°C)

## b) TZ (TrustZone) - thermal limits
## - Raise thermal trip points in TZ thermal policy
## - Increase CPU/GPU power budget

## c) ABL (Application Boot Loader)
## - Set performance mode in cmdline
## - Disable CPU power gating at boot
## - Enable all cores immediately

## d) XBL (eXtensible Boot Loader)
## - Configure PMIC for higher sustained power
## - Set DDR training for max bandwidth
